## Tasting Notes

Few wines demand attention the way the 2018 Palisades Petite Sirah does. Near-opaque in the glass, it unleashes a torrent of inky blackberry, black pepper, blueberry jam, violets, and iron. The palate is massive and structured — a wall of grainy, gripping tannin holds together an astonishing depth of black fruit, roasted espresso, dark chocolate, and garrigue. This is a wine that needs time, but rewards patience almost beyond measure.

> "A blockbuster that somehow maintains its composure. The density is extraordinary, yet the wine never loses its sense of place." — **93 Points**, Wine & Spirits

## The Palisades Block

This small, three-acre block clings to a steep, volcanic basalt face above the valley floor, facing southwest. Summer heat is intense, but the extreme drainage and ancient volcanic soils create a wine of almost architectural density with remarkable freshness underneath.

## Winemaking

45-day extended maceration. 28 months in new French and American oak. Assembled by hand, bottle-aged for an additional 12 months before release.
