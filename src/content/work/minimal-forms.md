## Tasting Notes

The 2019 Hillside Blend is an expression of mountain character at its most uncompromising. Deep garnet, almost opaque. The nose unfolds with iron, black olive tapenade, wild herbs, and a dark core of blackberry and dried fig. The palate is dense and brooding — volcanic tannins frame a powerful mid-palate of cassis, smoked meat, and bitter chocolate. The finish is almost austere in its length and precision.

> "Mountain intensity with classical restraint. The 2019 is a benchmark for the appellation — profound and cellar-worthy." — **96 Points**, Wine Spectator

## The Blend

- **68%** Cabernet Sauvignon
- **18%** Merlot
- **9%** Petit Verdot
- **5%** Cabernet Franc

## Viticulture

Howell Mountain sits above the fog line at 2,200 feet. The volcanic, iron-rich soils impart the telltale mountain tannin structure and deep mineral character. Our vines average 32 years in age.

## Winemaking

32-day maceration. 22 months in French and Hungarian oak (60% new). Assembled in January and bottled without filtration.

**Serve:** 62–65°F · **Decant:** 3+ hours · **Cellar:** 2025–2042
