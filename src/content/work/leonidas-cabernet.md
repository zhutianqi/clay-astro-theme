## Tasting Notes

The 2020 Leonidas opens with a dramatic cascade of crushed cassis, dark plum, and fresh violets, underscored by cedar, graphite, and a whisper of espresso. The full-bodied palate is at once powerful and precise — dense, velvety tannins carry an extraordinary depth of dark fruit across a finish that lingers for well over a minute.

This is a wine of immense presence and concentration, yet never heavy. The balance between fruit, acidity, and structure is a hallmark of the Leonidas philosophy: power in service of elegance.

> "Opens with gregarious blue and black fruits, dried herbs, and savory notes. The full-bodied palate is vibrant, expressive, mineral and earthy with black fruits and firm, grainy tannins, finishing long." — **98 Points**, Robert Parker, Wine Advocate

## Viticulture

Sourced from our oldest block on the eastern hillside, planted on volcanic, rocky soils at 1,400 feet elevation. Yields are deliberately restricted to under two tons per acre to concentrate flavors and extend hang time. The fruit is hand-harvested over multiple passes to ensure only peak-ripeness clusters are selected.

## Winemaking

Fermented in small, open-top tanks with extended maceration of 28 days. Aged for 24 months in 100% new French oak from the finest coopers in the Allier and Vosges forests. Unfined and unfiltered.

**Serve:** 60–65°F · **Decant:** 2 hours minimum · **Cellar:** 2024–2040
