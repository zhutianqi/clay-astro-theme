## Tasting Notes

The 2021 Reserve Chardonnay is a study in tension and grace. Pale gold in the glass, it opens with crushed flint, lemon curd, white peach, and a saline freshness reminiscent of sea spray. The palate is precise and crystalline — an electric acidity frames flavors of nectarine, Fuji apple, toasted almond, and white flowers, all culminating in a long, mineral-driven finish that fades slowly, like a note held at the end of a phrase.

> "Extraordinary complexity and finesse. This is a wine of great poise with a crystalline purity of fruit that is genuinely rare in today's market." — **95 Points**, James Suckling

## Viticulture

The fruit originates from three blocks planted on a foggy ridge overlooking the Pacific, where the diurnal temperature differential can exceed 50°F. Farmed biodynamically since 2012, the vines express a genuine sense of place — cool, coastal, and deeply mineral.

## Winemaking

Whole-cluster pressed to tank, then transferred to 30% new French oak for native fermentation. Weekly bâtonnage for six months builds texture without weight. No malolactic fermentation — the wine's bright acidity is its defining character.

**Serve:** 52–55°F · **Decant:** 30 minutes · **Cellar:** 2023–2032
