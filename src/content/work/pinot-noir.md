## Tasting Notes

The 2021 Estate Pinot Noir possesses a translucent ruby brilliance that hints at the delicacy within. The nose drifts between fresh strawberry, red cherry, and dried rose petal, before deeper notes of forest floor, cured meat, and a faint earthiness emerge. On the palate, it is ethereal — silken tannins and bright acidity carry layers of red fruit and spice to a finish that is long, fragrant, and deeply satisfying.

> "Effortlessly beautiful. The kind of Pinot Noir that reminds you why the grape is called the heartbreak grape — seductive and elusive in equal measure." — **94 Points**, Vinous

## Viticulture

Our Russian River block sits in a natural bowl where morning fog lingers until midday, keeping canopy temperatures cool throughout the growing season. The Goldridge sandy loam soils drain quickly, stressing the vines in the most beneficial way — concentrating flavors without surrendering aromatic lift.

## Winemaking

20% whole-cluster inclusion during fermentation. 10-day cold soak. 14 months in 30% new French Burgundian barrels. Bottled by gravity, unfined, unfiltered.

**Serve:** 58–62°F · **Decant:** 45 minutes · **Cellar:** 2023–2033
