## Elena Marchand

Elena Marchand grew up in Lyon and spent her early career in Bordeaux, working harvests at three premier cru classé estates before a chance encounter led her to a small Burgundy domaine where she spent four formative years understanding what it means to make wine in service of place rather than winemaker ego.

She joined Maison in 2011. She was 31. She has not left.

> "The best thing I can do for a wine is to make fewer decisions. The vineyard knows what it wants to be. My job is not to override it."

## Philosophy

Elena's approach is built on one conviction: the finest wines come from the finest farming. She has implemented full biodynamic certification across all estate blocks, introduced cover cropping between rows to build soil microbiology, and reduced irrigation to near zero in the Cabernet blocks.

In the cellar, she works with minimal intervention — native yeast fermentations only, no enzymes, no concentration techniques, no fining. She believes a wine should be the honest expression of a year in a place, not a manufactured product.
