## Frequently Asked Questions

---

**How do I purchase the wines?**

Most of our wines are sold through our allocation list. To join, please [contact us](/contact). We also sell through a small network of selected retail partners and fine dining establishments.

---

**What is the allocation list?**

Our allocation list gives members the first opportunity to purchase each new release before they are offered to the trade. List members receive an annual offer in the spring (for white wines and Pinot Noir) and in the autumn (for Cabernet-based wines). There is no minimum purchase requirement, and membership is free.

---

**Can I visit the estate?**

Yes, by appointment. We welcome guests for private tastings Thursday through Sunday. See our [Wine Tasting](/wine-tasting) page for details.

---

**Do you ship internationally?**

We ship to all US states where direct-to-consumer shipment is permitted by law. For international enquiries, please [contact us](/contact) — we can often arrange delivery through our international distribution partners.

---

**How should I store and serve the wines?**

All red wines should be stored horizontally in a cool, dark environment — ideally 55–58°F with moderate humidity. Our Cabernet-based wines benefit from a minimum of 2–3 hours of decanting before serving. Whites should be served between 52–56°F.

---

**I cannot afford your wines. Is there anything more accessible?**

We understand. Our Estate Pinot Noir and Reserve Chardonnay represent the most accessible entry points to the estate, and both over-deliver considerably at their price points. We are also exploring a second label — watch this space.

---

**Are the wines vegan?**

Yes. We do not fine our wines, and all cellar practices are animal-product free. Certified vegan from the 2018 vintage onward.
