## Less Is the Only Answer

We have observed, over three decades, that the finest vintages are not the ones where the winemaker was most active. They are the ones where the vineyard did its work and the cellar stayed out of the way.

This is our guiding principle. It sounds simple. It is extraordinarily hard to follow.

## On Biodynamics

We adopted biodynamic farming not because it is fashionable — it was not, when we started — but because it works. The soil health on our estate has improved measurably each decade. Vine root depth has increased. Canopy vigour is in balance. The wines taste more of the place they come from.

We do not ask you to believe in the metaphysical dimensions of biodynamic agriculture. We only ask you to taste the wines and draw your own conclusions.

## On Scores

Scores are a fact of commercial life in wine, and we are grateful when the critics respond to what we make. But we do not make wines to achieve scores. We make them to be drunk — ideally, with food, with people you love, on an evening that deserves a great bottle.

If our wines score 98 points and give you no pleasure, we have failed. If they score 88 points and you open the second bottle without thinking, we have succeeded.

## On Rarity

Our production is small and will remain small. We cannot serve everyone who wishes to purchase the wines. We are sorry for this. We work with an allocation system that prioritises our most loyal customers and the finest restaurants and merchants who represent our wines honestly.
