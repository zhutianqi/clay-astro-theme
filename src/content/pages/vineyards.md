## The Estate Blocks

All 42 acres of the Maison estate are farmed biodynamically and certified by Demeter. Each block is managed as a distinct entity, reflecting differences in soil type, aspect, and vine age that ultimately find expression in the finished wines.

---

### The Lion Block
**18 acres · Volcanic basalt · East-facing · 1,200 ft elevation**

The oldest and most prestigious block on the estate. Planted in 1989 on fractured volcanic basalt over clay subsoil. The east-facing aspect provides morning sun and afternoon shade — crucial for maintaining natural acidity in a warm climate. Source of the Leonidas Cabernet Sauvignon.

---

### The Ridge Block
**8 acres · Rocky loam · South-facing · 1,450 ft elevation**

The highest block on the property. Rocky, free-draining soils and intense afternoon exposure. Low yields — often below 1.5 tons per acre. Contributes the backbone of the Hillside Blend.

---

### The Fog Block
**6 acres · Goldridge sandy loam · West-facing**

Located at lower elevation where marine fog is common through July. The cool conditions preserve natural acidity and aromatic lift — ideal for our Chardonnay and Pinot Noir.

---

### The Stone Terrace
**5 acres · Alluvial gravel · Flat · Established 2004**

A unique flat block in a narrow valley with heavy gravel over clay pan. The drainage challenges stress the vines productively. Planted to Merlot and Petit Verdot for blending.

---

### The Palisades
**3 acres · Volcanic basalt face · Southwest-facing · Steep**

The most dramatic block on the estate — a nearly vertical face of ancient volcanic rock. Home to our Petite Sirah. Yields are almost absurdly small. The wine is almost absurdly concentrated.
