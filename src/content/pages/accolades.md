## Recognition

We share these not to boast, but because they represent independent confirmation of what we believe about our wines: that they belong among the finest small producers in California.

---

## 2020 Vintage

**Leonidas Cabernet Sauvignon 2020**
> "Opens with gregarious blue and black fruits, dried herbs, chargrill and savory notes. The full-bodied palate is vibrant, expressive, mineral, and earthy with black fruits and firm, grainy tannins, finishing long."
> **98 Points — Robert Parker, Wine Advocate**

---

## 2021 Vintage

**Reserve Chardonnay 2021**
> "Extraordinary complexity and finesse. A wine of great poise with a crystalline purity of fruit that is genuinely rare in today's market."
> **95 Points — James Suckling**

**Estate Pinot Noir 2021**
> "Effortlessly beautiful. Among the finest Pinot Noirs produced in California this decade."
> **94 Points — Vinous**

---

## 2019 Vintage

**Howell Mountain Hillside Blend 2019**
> "Mountain intensity with classical restraint. The 2019 is a benchmark for the appellation — profound and cellar-worthy."
> **96 Points — Wine Spectator**

---

## 2018 Vintage

**Palisades Petite Sirah 2018**
> "A blockbuster that somehow maintains its composure. The density is extraordinary, yet the wine never loses its sense of place."
> **93 Points — Wine & Spirits**

**Leonidas Cabernet Sauvignon 2018**
> "One of the most complete expressions of Napa Valley Cabernet I have encountered in years. The 2018 will drink magnificently from 2026 through at least 2040."
> **97 Points — James Suckling**
