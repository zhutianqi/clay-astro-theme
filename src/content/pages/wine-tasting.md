## Tasting at the Estate

We offer private seated tastings in the estate's stone tasting room, overlooking the Lion Block vineyard. These are not large events. We welcome a maximum of eight guests per session and work by appointment only.

A standard tasting proceeds through four to six wines, guided by either our winemaker or a senior member of our hospitality team. We move slowly. We encourage questions. We never watch the clock.

---

## What to Expect

**The Welcome** — Arrival at the estate, a brief orientation to the property and its history, a glass of something cold from the library.

**The Tasting** — A seated, guided progression through current releases and, when available, library wines. Poured with context: the vintage conditions, the specific block, the decisions made in the cellar.

**The Conversation** — Every tasting is a dialogue. We want to know what you taste, what you think, what you're curious about.

**The Close** — For guests who wish to purchase wine, orders can be placed directly at the estate. For allocation wines, we can discuss joining the list.

---

## Booking

Tastings are available Thursday through Sunday, 10:00am – 4:00pm, by appointment only.

- **Standard Tasting** — $85 per person (4 wines, 90 minutes)
- **Winemaker Tasting** — $185 per person (6 wines + library selection, 2.5 hours, available select Saturdays)
