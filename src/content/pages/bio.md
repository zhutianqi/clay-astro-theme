## A Generation Devoted to the Vine

Maison was founded in 1989 by Thomas and Catherine Renard on a 42-acre parcel of hillside land above the Napa Valley floor. What began as a small Cabernet Sauvignon planting has grown — slowly, deliberately — into one of the valley's most respected small producers.

The philosophy has never changed: grow less, grow better. Our total annual production rarely exceeds 2,500 cases, and we intend to keep it that way. Every decision in the vineyard and the cellar is made in service of the wine, never the other way around.

![The Barrel Room](/img/wine-cellar-bottles.jpg)

## The Winemaker

Elena Marchand joined Maison in 2011 after a decade of formative work in Bordeaux and Burgundy. She brought with her a deep respect for restraint — a conviction that the finest wines are not made, but guided. Under her stewardship, the wines have achieved a new level of precision and complexity while retaining the power and generosity that define the estate.

Elena farms all blocks biodynamically and has introduced practices — whole-cluster fermentation for the Pinot Noir, extended maceration for the Cabernet — that have elevated the wines to their current standing.

## Our Commitment

We do not chase scores, though the scores arrive anyway. We do not seek volume, though demand continues to grow. What we seek, vintage after vintage, is the same thing our founders sought: a wine that is unmistakably from this place, made in this way, by these people — a wine that carries within it the full weight of a year's labour and the quiet ambition of a lifetime.

*We are grateful you are here.*
