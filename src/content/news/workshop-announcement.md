## Below the Surface

Beneath the estate house lies a cellar that was carved from the hillside in 1947. Its walls are lined with hand-set stone, and its temperature has remained a constant 56°F through every summer and winter since. It is here that our wines spend their most formative years — in silence, in darkness, in oak.

We now offer private guided tours of the cellar for collectors and enthusiasts who want to understand wine not as a beverage, but as a living, evolving thing.

Each **Private Cellar Experience** is customised to your interests and can include:

1. **A guided tour** of the barrel room, cave system, and library cellar
2. **Vertical tasting** of a single wine across 6–8 vintages
3. **Library dinner** — wines from our private archive, many not commercially available
4. **A conversation with the winemaker** — available on selected dates

### Booking Information

- **Duration:** 3–5 hours depending on format
