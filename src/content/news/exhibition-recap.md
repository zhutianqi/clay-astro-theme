## An Unforgettable Evening

On the last evening of harvest, as the final bins of Cabernet were brought in from the hillside block, we gathered at long tables under the oaks to celebrate. The 2024 vintage was, by every measure, exceptional — cool mornings, dry afternoons, and a long, slow October that allowed the fruit to reach a complexity rarely seen here.

Winemaker Elena Marchand opened the evening with a vertical of our Leonidas Cabernet spanning 2015–2023, inviting guests to trace the arc of a decade's work in the vineyard and cellar. Each bottle told a different story, yet all shared the same unmistakable signature: precision, depth, and a sense of place that no amount of technique can manufacture.

The menu, composed by our culinary partner Chef Olivier Dubois, wove through eight courses — each paired to showcase a different aspect of the vintage. Highlights included a slow-braised short rib alongside the 2019 Hillside Blend and a beautifully aged Comté with the 2021 Reserve Chardonnay.

We are deeply grateful to the friends, collectors, and trade partners who joined us. The 2024 vintage will be released to our allocation list members in Spring 2025.
