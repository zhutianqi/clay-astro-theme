## Walk the Vines Before They Bear Fruit

There is a stillness in the vineyard in spring that is entirely different from harvest. The vines are just beginning to push, tender green shoots emerging from ancient wood. The air smells of damp earth and wild mustard. It is, in many ways, the most honest time to visit.

On **Saturday, June 8th**, we invite a small group of guests — limited to 16 — for a guided morning walk through our estate blocks with head viticulturist Marco Pellini. Marco will discuss our biodynamic farming practices, our approach to canopy management, and what the early signs of the 2024 growing season suggest.

Following the walk, guests will be seated in the barrel room for a tasting of wines currently in élevage:

- **2023 Leonidas Cabernet Sauvignon** — 8 months in barrel
- **2023 Hillside Blend** — components separately, then assembled for the first time
- **2023 Reserve Chardonnay** — pre-bâtonnage and post-bâtonnage comparison
